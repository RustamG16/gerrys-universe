1. WHAT RUSTAM OFFERS (page "Your edge")
=====================================================================
Target client: owners who have run several businesses for years and are now moving into the digital era.

Pillar 1 — One partner, not many providers.
Instead of separate contacts for web design, development, automation/IT, hosting and domains,
marketing and social, booking and CRM tools (6 contacts, 6 briefings, 6 invoices), the owner has
one contact, one briefing, one plan. The simplification is the product.

Pillar 2 — High-end custom, not templates.
Custom, cinematic, scroll-animated design built around the brand, not a WordPress theme.
Grounded in psychology (trust and decisions), human-centered computing (designed and tested
around real users) and design principles (CRAP: contrast, repetition, alignment, proximity).
Proof: the Marina Fior website, built in 2 working days (real build time, stated by Rustam).

Pillar 3 — Powered by the Convenium system.
Rustam is the single contact. Behind him is a repeatable system: AI workflow, a design framework
(Apollo), documented templates and SOPs, and quality checks (desktop, mobile, both languages,
accessibility). It is "Rustam + system": not a solo freelancer, not a big agency with overhead.
Because the work is documented and reusable, it holds up as more clients arrive.

Gerry's three businesses: Marina Fior (psychology practice), Active Relaxing (apartments in
Schladming and Carinthia, Austria), Villa Olivija (apartments in Stara Novalja, Croatia).

2. THE PROPOSED DEAL (page "Partnership")
=====================================================================
Role: Rustam is employed by Gerry's (Austrian) company as Digitalisation Manager.
Contract: permanent, full-time.
Base salary: EUR 3,465 gross per month x 14 payments = EUR 48,510 gross per year.
  - This is the legal minimum for the visa category below. Rustam deliberately accepts the
    minimum base to keep Gerry's fixed cost as low as possible; his upside comes from commission.
  - Estimated full employer cost incl. ~30% employer contributions: about EUR 63,000 per year
    (estimate — confirm with payroll).

Two kinds of work:
  A) Gerry's own businesses (Marina Fior, Active Relaxing, Villa Olivija): salary only.
  B) Network projects: new clients from Gerry's network (expected: multi-business owners who need
     unification and automation). Gerry opens the door, Gerry's company signs and invoices,
     Rustam delivers. Salary + commission.

Commission (network projects only), on NET project revenue (revenue minus direct project costs
such as freelancers, software, ads), per contract year, bands reset every contract year:
  - First EUR 100,000: Rustam 10% / Gerry 90%  (Gerry recovers the salary cost first)
  - Above EUR 100,000: Rustam 70% / Gerry 30%
  - Paid quarterly, after the client has paid. Rustam may see invoices and costs of commissioned projects.
Worked examples (yearly, before employer contributions on the commission and before tax):
  - EUR 50,000 revenue:  Rustam commission 5,000;  Gerry share 45,000;  Gerry after salary cost ~ -18,000
  - EUR 100,000 revenue: Rustam commission 10,000; Gerry share 90,000;  Gerry after salary cost ~ 27,000
  - EUR 200,000 revenue: Rustam commission 80,000; Gerry share 120,000; Gerry after salary cost ~ 57,000
  - Gerry's share covers the full salary cost at about EUR 70,000 network revenue per year.
  Note: commission is paid as salary, so employer contributions on it come on top. The salary also
  pays for setting up Gerry's own three businesses, which earns nothing in this calculation.

Ownership:
  - Rustam keeps his system: Convenium design system (Apollo), components, templates and code base,
    AI agents, prompts and workflows, Notion OS templates and SOPs, and every improvement to the system.
  - Gerry owns everything made for him: his websites and apps (the finished products), texts, photos
    and videos made for him, his Notion workspace and all data, domains, accounts, customer contacts,
    his brands and logos.
  - Gerry gets a free, permanent licence to use the system parts inside his products, even if the
    collaboration ends.

Side activity: Rustam may run Convenium on a small scale alongside the job; no non-compete.

Timeline (months from signing): contract and visa ~0-2 (decision within 8 weeks) → setup of Gerry's
three businesses ~2-11 → network projects ~9 onward → after 21 of 24 months of employment Rustam can
switch to the Red-White-Red Card plus (free labour-market access). After that the collaboration can
continue as a partnership, e.g. Gerry refers clients for a 30% share — terms to agree then.

3. VISA — RED-WHITE-RED CARD FOR OTHER KEY WORKERS (Gerry's part)
=====================================================================
Rustam is a non-EU citizen currently in Austria on a student residence permit (studies not yet
finished). The proposed route is the Red-White-Red Card for "other key workers" (sonstige Schlüsselkräfte).
Requirements (source: oesterreich.gv.at, checked 12 Oct 2026):
  - at least 55 points (qualification, work experience, language, age);
  - gross salary at least 50% of the maximum social-insurance contribution base (≈ EUR 3,465/month
    in 2026, plus special payments);
  - labour market test: the regional AMS office checks whether a registered jobseeker could fill the
    role — the job description must be concrete and specific.
Gerry's steps: (1) sign a written job offer (permanent, full-time, Digitalisation Manager, salary);
(2) sign the employer declaration under the AuslBG; (3) the application is filed at the residence
authority (the employer may file it); fee EUR 218; (4) AMS check; (5) decision within 8 weeks;
work starts only after the card is collected. The card is valid up to 2 years and tied to the employer.

## New venture — Group experience + travel

Working concept: professional group sessions, accommodation and restorative destination activities offered as a carefully scoped retreat. This is a proposed business idea, not an established programme or treatment promise.

**Three test formats:**

1. **Austria Reset:** 3 days in Carinthia; facilitated reflection, gentle nature time, optional individual administrative inquiry and local experience partners.
2. **Adriatic Escape:** 5 days near Stara Novalja; a slower seasonal stay with group sessions, coastal activities and protected free time.
3. **B2B Reset:** 2–3 day team offsite; organisational wellbeing education and team reflection, within confirmed professional scope. No employee diagnosis or mandatory personal disclosure.

**Validation sequence:** 10 audience interviews → interest page and voluntary waitlist → clinical/professional, commercial and logistical review → costed small paid pilot → feedback and unit economics → decide to repeat, revise or stop. Do not collect health history in an ordinary marketing waitlist.

**Package definition before launch:** named provider, professional scope, destination, exact dates, capacity, agenda, accommodation, meals, transfers, insurance responsibilities, accessibility, cancellation/refund rules and participant eligibility. Have the appropriate professional advisers review the structure and travel/clinical responsibilities.

**Illustrative economic model:** attendees × price per person = gross revenue. Contribution before taxes = attendees × (price − variable cost per attendee) − fixed costs. Break-even = ceiling(fixed costs ÷ positive unit contribution). If unit contribution is zero/negative, there is no break-even under those assumptions. Accommodation opportunity cost, facilitator pay, marketing, food, transport, provider fees, refunds and contingency must be included. These are inputs to validate, not forecasts.

## Marketing system

**Journey:** Discover → Trust → Enquire → Book → Return. Every content item has a useful next step and traceable destination.

**Six proposed social accounts:** Marina Fior; Active Relaxing; a disclosed Active Relaxing AI destination creator; Villa Olivija; a disclosed Villa AI destination creator; the new retreat concept. Six is a launch hypothesis from the notes, not a requirement to operate all six immediately. Protect capacity by staging the main three first and validating the others.

**Three vlogs:** psychology education, Austrian destination experiences, Adriatic destination stories. Useful website transcripts and topic articles can support search visibility; create accurate, original content.

**Two launch films:** an initial curiosity film connecting destinations and wellbeing, then a reveal film introducing each brand and the appropriate next action. Generate visual concepts for planning; use confirmed property and practitioner material where factual depiction matters.

**Proposed monthly cadence:** each established brand 4 short videos, 4 feed posts and weekly stories; each AI destination account 2 reviewed editorial clips monthly after validation; retreat content begins with audience questions and waitlist research. Adjust output after effort and inquiry quality are measured.

**Campaign pillars:** people and trust; places and experiences; practical planning; transparent offers; returning guest/client education. Measure inquiry quality and confirmed outcomes rather than likes alone.

**Quarterly loop:** research and baseline → one focused experiment per brand → review results → improve pages/content → document what worked → reuse it. Ads require separate budget, platform access, approved assets and defined conversion events.

## Eight-module operating system

Start with the clearest pain. Customers: leads, follow-up and quotes. Money: invoices, expenses and contribution. Automation: confirmations, reminders, reports. Team: tasks, onboarding and SOPs. Online: websites, booking and content. AI assistant: reviewed drafts, translation and summaries. Plans: goals, ventures and documents. Security: access, backups and support. A shared visual overview does not require merging sensitive data.

**Proposed Notion structure:** owner overview; one business project per brand; shared content/asset library; goals/KPIs; task board; marketing experiments; SOPs; decisions; integration inventory; retreat pilot.
